This is the official privacy policy for Air Fryer Recipe Book by G. Thompson.

The same policy is shown in the app under Settings → Legal → Privacy Policy and is the policy linked from the Google Play Store listing.

A browser-friendly copy of this policy is published at:
https://gjt7976.github.io/app-privacy-policies/air-fryer-recipe-book.html

Air Fryer Recipe Book ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

This app operates entirely offline and on your device.

We do not collect, store on any server, transmit, or share any personal data.

No account is required. No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs are embedded, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves your preferences and content locally on your device so they are available the next time you open the app.

This may include:

  • Shopping lists
  • Favourite recipes
  • Collections
  • Recipe ratings
  • Personal notes
  • Meal plans
  • Unit preferences
  • Theme settings
  • Language settings
  • A local record indicating whether Meal Planner Pro has been purchased

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

You can remove locally stored information by clearing applicable lists or content within the app, restoring a backup, clearing the app's data through your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

If you use the Back up / Export feature, the app writes your data to a file in a location you choose. That exported file is then under your control.

Purchases

Meal Planner Pro is sold through Google Play Billing on Android.

Purchases and refunds are processed by Google according to Google's terms and privacy policies. Air Fryer Recipe Book does not receive or store your credit card number, banking information, or other payment details.

The app only records locally whether the Meal Planner Pro unlock is active.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics services, data brokers, or other third-party tracking services.

Share, Email, and Print features are performed using services provided by your device's operating system.

Website and support links open using your device's browser or email application.

Children's Privacy

Air Fryer Recipe Book does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The app contains no age-restricted content and does not collect personal data from users of any age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the app and on this published Privacy Policy page.
