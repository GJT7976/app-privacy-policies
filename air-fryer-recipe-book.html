Thompson. What the App stores on your device: Dog, breed, health, genetic-test, pedigree, welfare-review and temperament records you enter Breeding, litter, puppy, buyer-contact and expense records you enter Photographs and documents you attach to those records (non-Web platforms only) Your Settings choices (language, purchases/entitlements, and similar app preferences) What the App does not do: it does not upload your records to a server, does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not share your data with third parties for advertising. Your controls: you can export your data, create a backup, restore from a backup, and delete some or all of your data at any time from Settings — see the Data Backup & Data Loss Disclaimer and the User Data Deletion Policy. Children's privacy: Canine Legacy Breeder App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, since all data entry is local and manual by the app's adult user. International users: because storage is local to your device, your data does not cross international borders through the App itself. Questions about this policy can be directed to G. Thompson through the Google Play store listing for Canine Legacy Breeder App. © G.
