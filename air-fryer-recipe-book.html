Privacy Policy — Air Fryer Recipe Book Effective: January 1, 2024 Revised: 2026 This is the official privacy policy for Air Fryer Recipe Book by G. Thompson. The same text is shown in the app under Settings → Legal → Privacy Policy and is the policy linked from the Google Play Store listing. Air Fryer Recipe Book ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. Information We Collect This app operates entirely offline and on your device. We do not collect, store on any server, transmit, or share any personal data. No account is required. No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs are embedded, and the app makes no network requests of its own. Data Stored on Your Device The app saves your preferences and content locally on your device so they are available the next time you open it. This includes: Shopping lists Favourite recipes Collections Recipe ratings Personal notes Meal plans Unit settings Theme settings Language settings Whether Meal Planner Pro has been purchased This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove locally stored information at any time by clearing applicable information within the app, clearing the app's data through your device settings, or uninstalling the app. If you use the Back up / Export feature, the app writes this data to a file in a location you choose. That exported file is then under your control. Purchases Meal Planner Pro is sold through Google Play Billing on Android. Purchases and refunds are handled by Google according to Google's terms and privacy policies. Air Fryer Recipe Book does not receive or store your credit card number, banking information, or other payment details. The app only records locally whether the Meal Planner Pro unlock is active. Third-Party Services The app does not integrate with advertising networks, analytics services, data brokers, or other third-party tracking services. Share, Email, and Print features are performed using services provided by your device's operating system. Website and support links open using your device's browser or email application. Children's Privacy Air Fryer Recipe Book does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no age-restricted content and does not collect personal data from users of any age. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
