Privacy Policy

POV POS is an offline-first point-of-sale and small-business management app built by G Thompson / Niagara Indie Apps. This policy explains what happens to data when a business uses it — matched line-for-line to what the app actually does, not a generic template. https://gjt7976.github.io/app-privacy-policies/posprivacy-policy.html

01 Summary

POV POS is designed to run entirely on your own device. We do not operate a server that receives your business data, we do not collect analytics, and we do not show advertising.

02 Where your data lives

Everything you enter — products, recipes, inventory, sales, receipts, employees, timekeeping, payroll figures, accounting records, and settings — is stored locally in a database on the device running the app. We, the developer, do not have access to this data. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party by the app.

03 Local network (Host / Client) mode

POV POS offers an optional feature that lets one device ("Host") share its product catalogue and accept sales from other devices ("Client") over the same local Wi-Fi network or a phone's own personal hotspot. This connection:

  • stays entirely within your local network — it does not use the internet and does not pass through any server we operate;
  • is protected by a one-time pairing code and an authenticated session token generated on your device;
  • is used only to synchronise product, price, and stock data and to submit sales between your own registers.

This feature is optional and off by default — the app starts in Standalone mode.

04 Backups

The app can create a backup file of your business data, optionally protected with a passphrase you choose. Backup files are saved to your device's own storage, or to a location you explicitly pick to save a copy to. We do not receive a copy of any backup.

05 What we do not do

  • We do not include advertising or ad-network SDKs.
  • We do not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that transmit data off the device.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your business data with third parties — because we never receive it in the first place.
  • We do not require you to create an account with us to use the app.

06 Permissions

On Android, the app requests:

PermissionWhy
Internet, network state, Wi-Fi state Used only for the optional local Host/Client feature above — running a local server on your network and letting other registers connect to it. Never used to send data to us or to any third party.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or any other sensitive Android permission.

07 Children's privacy

POV POS is a business tool intended for business owners and their staff. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

08 Canadian tax and payroll data

Business identifiers you optionally enter for Canadian tax/payroll purposes — such as a CRA Business Number, GST/HST number, or payroll program account number — are stored locally with the rest of your business data under the same terms as above. They are not transmitted anywhere by the app.

09 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same location with a new effective date.
