Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026

Publisher: G. Thompson

This is the official privacy policy for Hummus House. The same text is shown inside the app under Settings → Legal → Privacy Policy, and this page is the policy linked from the Google Play store listing.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hummus House app handles information. In short, the App is designed to work offline and does not run its own accounts or servers. https://gjt7976.github.io/app-privacy-policies/hummus-house.html

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or contacts. Content you create in the App — saved recipes, collections, shopping lists, meal plans, ratings and notes — is stored only on your device.

Purchases

If you buy Hummus House Pro, the purchase is processed by the platform app store, such as Google Play. We receive confirmation that the purchase is valid so we can unlock the feature. We do not receive your payment card details. The store's own privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Diagnostics

The App does not include third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. If crash reporting or basic diagnostics are enabled in a future version, they will be limited to non-identifying technical data, disclosed here, and consistent with the store's data-safety declaration.

Backups you create

When you export a backup file, it contains your in-app content in readable JSON. You choose where that file goes, such as another app or storage location. Anyone with the file can read it, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Your controls

  • Delete individual items at any time within the App.
  • Use Settings → Data → Delete all data to erase everything stored on the device.
  • Uninstalling the App removes its local data from the device.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the App and on this published page.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@hummushouse.app.
