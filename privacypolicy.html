Privacy Policy

Bar Tender Atlas

Bar Tender Atlas is designed to work fully offline. G. Thompson does not operate a server for this app and does not collect, transmit or sell your personal data.

What is stored, and where

All app data — your favorites, ratings, personal notes, collections, “My Bar” ingredients, shopping list, custom recipes, and settings — is stored only on your device using local storage. It is never uploaded to us.

What we do NOT collect

No account is required. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos library, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. The app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Photos

If you add a photo to a custom recipe, the image is copied into the app's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Purchases

The optional one-time “Atlas Pro” upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. Google processes the transaction under its own privacy policy; the app receives only a confirmation that the product is owned. No payment details reach the app or G. Thompson.

Backups and exports

Backup and export files are created only when you ask for them and are shared through your device's own share sheet to a destination you choose.

Children

The app is intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed to children.

Your control

You can delete all app data at any time from Settings → Data → Delete all data.
