Privacy Policy — Ramen Recipes

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Ramen Recipes (“the app”) is published by G Thompson. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

Summary. The app collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no cloud services.

Information the app stores on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally on your device only. It is never transmitted anywhere and is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Which recipes you have marked as favourites.
  • Your settings: theme (dark / light / system), language, and measurement units (Metric / US Customary).
  • Whether you have purchased the optional “Ramen Recipes Pro” unlock.
  • (Pro) any personal notes or collections you create.

Purchases

The optional one-time “Ramen Recipes Pro” unlock is processed by Google Play Billing. The app never sees your payment details. Google’s handling of that transaction is covered by Google’s own privacy policy. The app only records, on your device, whether the unlock is active.

Network access

The app requests the INTERNET permission solely so that Google Play Billing can complete a purchase or restore. No recipe content, images, analytics or diagnostics are downloaded or uploaded. The app is fully usable with no network connection.

Permissions

  • Internet — used only for the optional in-app purchase / restore, via Google Play Billing.

The app requests no other permissions (no location, camera, contacts, storage, microphone, notifications, or device identifiers).

Children

The app contains no user-generated content, no chat, no ads and no data collection. It is suitable for a general audience.

Data deletion

All app data is local. To delete it, uninstall the app, or use Settings → Apps → Ramen Recipes → Storage → Clear data. There is no server-side data to delete because none is collected.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new “Last updated” date.
