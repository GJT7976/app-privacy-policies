Bread Maker — Privacy Policy

Baker's Percentage Calculator  ·  package com.breadmaker.app

Effective date: 7 September 2026

Summary. Bread Maker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. https://gjt7976.github.io/app-privacy-policies/breadmakerp-policy.html

Data we collect

None. The app has no servers of its own and does not send your information anywhere. We have no way to identify you or contact you through the app.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using your device's app storage (localStorage), so your work is still there next time you open it:

  • Recipes you choose to save (ingredient percentages, names, and any notes you add)
  • Custom presets you create
  • Baking timers you start (label and finish time)
  • App settings: theme, language, weight unit, temperature unit

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see below). You can erase all of it at any time from your device's Settings → Apps → Bread Maker → Storage → Clear data, or by removing individual recipes and timers inside the app.

Actions you can take that involve other apps

  • Download backup — creates a .json file containing your saved recipes and settings and hands it to your device's file/download handling. The file goes only where you choose to put it.
  • Share — passes a plain-text version of a recipe to your device's standard share sheet. You pick the destination; the app does not choose one or send anything on its own.
  • Print / Save as PDF — hands a formatted recipe page to your device's system print service. Choosing "Save as PDF" there writes a file wherever you select.

Each of these happens only when you tap the corresponding button.

Permissions

The app requests only the INTERNET permission. It is used solely so the app's own interface files can be cached for offline use the first time the app runs. The app does not load remote content, ads, or analytics, and works fully offline after installation.

Analytics, advertising, and cookies

The app uses none of these. There are no usage-tracking SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no cookies.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because no personal data is collected, there are no special restrictions for use by minors.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how the app handles information, the effective date above will be updated and the revised policy distributed with the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's developer through the Google Play store listing's developer contact details.

Bread Maker is published by G Thompson. This policy applies to version 1.1.0 and later of the Android application com.breadmaker.app.
